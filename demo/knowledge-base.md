# Skyline Renovations — services, pricing and terms

## About us

Skyline Renovations carries out turnkey renovation of flats and houses. We have
worked in the city since 2014 and complete around forty projects a year. All
work is carried out by our own crews; we do not subcontract structural or
installation work.

## Pricing

Prices are per square metre of the flat's total area.

**Rough work — from 520 EUR/m²**
Demolition, waste removal, electrical rewiring, plumbing rough-in, screed,
wall levelling and plastering to a paint-ready finish.

**Finishing work — from 340 EUR/m²**
Painting, wallpapering, floor laying, tiling, fitting doors, skirting and
sockets.

**Full turnkey — from 860 EUR/m²**
Both of the above. A two-room flat of 54 m² therefore starts at approximately
46,000 EUR for labour.

Flats above the fourth floor in buildings without a service lift carry a
haulage surcharge of 15 EUR/m².

Bathrooms and kitchens are priced separately from the rest of the flat because
of the density of plumbing and tiling work. A standard bathroom of up to 5 m²
starts at 4,200 EUR including waterproofing.

## What the price includes

- Site survey and a written estimate before any work begins
- Protection of entrance doors, windows and lifts
- Daily cleaning and removal of construction waste
- A foreman assigned to your project as the single point of contact
- Photo reports at the end of each stage

## What the price does not include

- Materials — these are billed separately, see below
- Furniture, appliances, curtains and light fittings
- Removal or alteration of load-bearing walls, which requires a structural
  survey and council approval
- Design and technical drawings

## Materials

Materials are bought at cost with no markup from us. Before work starts we
provide a purchase list with quantities and specifications, and you buy directly
from the supplier. This keeps the receipts in your name and the choice yours.

If you would rather not handle ordering and deliveries, we can manage
procurement for a handling fee of 10 percent of the material cost.

Typical material spend for turnkey work is 40 to 60 percent of the labour cost,
depending on the finishes chosen.

## Timelines

A two-room flat of around 54 m² takes eight to ten weeks from the start of
demolition to handover. A one-room flat takes six to eight weeks. Houses are
quoted individually after the survey.

The schedule is agreed in writing before work begins and is broken into stages
with dates. If we fall behind for reasons within our control, the penalty terms
are set out in the contract.

Delays caused by late material deliveries are outside our control. We will tell
you as soon as we know, and we do not charge for time we did not work.

## Payment

Work is paid in three stages:

- 30 percent on signing the contract
- 40 percent at the halfway inspection
- 30 percent on handover

The final payment is due only after you have walked the site with the foreman
and signed the handover document. We accept bank transfer and card.

## Warranty

All structural and installation work carries a two year warranty. Decorative
finishes carry a one year warranty.

The warranty covers workmanship. It does not cover damage caused by use, by
flooding from neighbours, or by other contractors working on the property after
we hand it over.

Warranty claims are answered within three working days and remedial work is
scheduled within two weeks.

## Working in an occupied flat

We can work while you are living in the flat. It extends the schedule by roughly
two weeks and dust control becomes the main constraint, so we seal off work
areas and run the messiest stages consecutively rather than in parallel.

Pets must be kept out of work areas during demolition and screed pouring, both
for their safety and because doors are open for long periods.

## The survey

The site survey is free anywhere within the city and takes about an hour. The
surveyor measures the flat, discusses what you want, and photographs the current
condition. You receive a written estimate within three working days.

The estimate is fixed for 30 days and does not change unless the scope does.
